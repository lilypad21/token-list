# Investigation: Mueller Investigative Services / Simone Mueller

**Compiled:** 2026-08-09
**Scope:** Open-source (public web) research across ten sources
**Subject:** Mueller Investigative Services, LLC and its owner, Simone M. Mueller

---

## Summary

Mueller Investigative Services, LLC is an active California limited liability
company providing private investigative services. It is owned and operated by
Simone M. Mueller, who is also the LLC's registered agent. The firm is
registered in Orange County (Mission Viejo) and advertises a second operating
address in Carlsbad (San Diego County), holding California private investigator
license **PI 27441**. The business presents itself as a small, owner-operated
practice serving Southern California, and has at least one documented
institutional client engagement (a charter school organization) in 2025.

## Positioning and services

The firm's own website describes it as a **"professional, reliable, and
confidential"** private investigations business offering services "for all
investigative needs in California." The public marketing copy is generalist —
it does not name a specialty vertical such as insurance defense, surveillance,
or domestic work.

The one concrete engagement found in public records points toward
**institutional / organizational investigations** rather than consumer work:

> An agreement dated **Monday, June 30, 2025** between Mueller Investigative
> Services, LLC and **American Indian Model Schools** — an Oakland, California
> charter school organization — surfaced in AIM Schools' public
> BoardOnTrack document portal.

American Indian Model Schools is a charter operator that was in the middle of a
contested charter-renewal process in 2025 (Alameda County granted it a renewal
lifeline in March 2025). An engagement of an outside investigator by a charter
board in that period is most consistent with a personnel, compliance, or
governance investigation, though **the document contents could not be retrieved
to confirm the nature of the work.**

This is the single most informative data point in the record: it suggests the
practice's real-world work skews toward **workplace / organizational
investigations for public-sector and nonprofit boards**, a materially different
market from the generic "all investigative needs" framing on the website.

## Discrepancies and gaps

These are flagged rather than resolved, because sources genuinely conflict:

1. **Formation date conflict.** California Secretary of State records give a
   registration date of **March 7, 2021**. One aggregator citing BBB reports a
   business start date of **July 11, 2011**. Both can be true: a sole
   proprietorship or DBA operating from 2011 that was later reorganized as an
   LLC in 2021. If the ten-year operating history is accurate, the LLC's
   formation date understates the practice's actual tenure — worth correcting
   wherever the 2021 date is the only thing a client sees.

2. **Two addresses, two counties.** Orange County (registered) and San Diego
   County (licensed/advertised). No source explains the relationship — whether
   this is a relocation, a deliberate two-market presence, or a stale listing.

3. **License number unverified at source.** License 27441 appears in a
   third-party document, but the California Bureau of Security and Investigative
   Services (BSIS) verification portal was not reachable from this environment
   to confirm status, issue date, or expiration. **Recommend verifying directly**
   at `bsis.ca.gov` — an expired or lapsed listing is the kind of thing that
   quietly breaks client onboarding.

4. **No professional footprint found.** No LinkedIn profile, no CALI (California
   Association of Licensed Investigators) directory listing, no PInow profile,
   no press mentions, no prior-career or credential history (e.g. law
   enforcement background) appeared in any search. For a licensed PI, CALI
   membership and a PInow listing are the two standard referral channels; their
   absence is the most visible gap in the online presence.

5. **Website platform.** The public site is a legacy Google Sites page. It
   returned only a one-line description to indexers, which is why searches
   surface third-party aggregators (BBB, bizprofile) above the firm's own site.

## Name collisions to be aware of

Searches on "Mueller" plus "investigation" are heavily polluted. The following
are **unrelated** entities and should not be conflated with this subject:

- **Robert S. Mueller III / the Mueller special counsel investigation** —
  dominates nearly every search; unrelated federal matter.
- **Mueller Security & Investigations** — Buffalo, New York.
- **Mueller Services, Inc. / Mueller Reports** — Tonawanda, New York; insurance
  inspection services. Carries a significant volume of BBB and ComplaintsBoard
  complaints, including employment-practice allegations. **This reputational
  baggage is not the subject's**, but the name similarity means it can be
  mistaken for it by a client doing a casual search.
- **Muller Investigation Services, Inc.** — Florida (P15000073650).
- **Matthew Muller**, **Jack Muller**, **Martin U. Müller** — unrelated
  individuals.

The Mueller Services / Mueller Reports collision is the practical risk here: a
prospective client searching "Mueller investigative services reviews" will land
on a page of complaints belonging to a different company in another state.
